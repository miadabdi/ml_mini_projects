My fellow Americans,

Tonight, I want to talk about the future of our nation. We face many challenges, but also incredible opportunities.

One of the greatest opportunities lies in the field of technology. We are investing in American innovation. We are building the next generation of semiconductor chips right here in Ohio. We are ensuring that artificial intelligence is developed safely and responsibly, to help us cure diseases and combat climate change, not to undermine our democracy.

We are also expanding high-speed internet to every corner of this country, because in the 21st century, high-speed internet is not a luxury, it is a necessity.

Let us move forward together, embracing the future with confidence and hope.

God bless you all, and may God protect our troops.
